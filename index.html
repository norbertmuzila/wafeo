WAFEO is a digital twin of water, agriculture and food systems. It turns open satellite data from Copernicus, NASA and partners into crop, water and food-security intelligence, from a single field to a whole continent.

The stakes

Harvests are counted once a season.
Satellites watch every week.

By the time a failed harvest shows up in the statistics, the window to act has closed. Earth observation sees crop and water stress while there is still time to respond.

About

We built WAFEO on a simple belief: every farmer, water manager and ministry deserves to see what the satellites see, in time to act on it.

WAFEO joins open data from Copernicus, NASA, FAO, WFP and partners into one digital twin of water, agriculture and food systems, with a focus on Africa and coverage across every continent.
